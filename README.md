# Neon and Beyond — 2026 Cities

Interactive nocturnal ASCII skyline gallery celebrating the 6 cities Google Cloud traveled through in 2026 — **Seoul, Dubai, Singapore, Taipei, Beijing, and Bangalore**.

Built with **Gemini 4 Neon**.

## Cities
- **Seoul (`SEOUL · 서울`)** — Cheomseongdae, Korean pine, N Seoul Tower, Namdaemun, Bukchon Hanok, King Sejong statue, Baekja moon jar, cranes, Han River sailboat & fireworks
- **Dubai (`DUBAI`)** — Arabian sand dunes, Museum of the Future, Burj Khalifa LED show & sky lasers, Emirates Towers, Dubai Frame, Burj Al Arab
- **Singapore (`SINGAPORE`)** — Marina Bay CBD, Esplanade domes, Marina Bay Sands SkyPark, ArtScience Lotus, Supertrees, Singapore Flyer, fireflies
- **Taipei (`TAIPEI · 台北`)** — Miramar Wheel, TPAC sphere, CKS Memorial Hall, Nanshan Plaza, Taipei 101, Grand Hotel, Elephant Mountain, Pingxi sky lanterns
- **Beijing (`BEIJING · 北京`)** — Great Wall, Summer Palace, Temple of Heaven, Forbidden City, Bird's Nest, auspicious clouds, crimson-gold fireworks
- **Bangalore (`BANGALORE · ಬೆಂಗಳೂರು`)** — Dravidian temple gopuram, Vidhana Soudha, Indo-Saracenic pavilion, UB City spire, coconut palm & banyan silhouettes, Diwali diyas

## Build & Run

```bash
python3 scripts/build_cities.py
open index.html
```
